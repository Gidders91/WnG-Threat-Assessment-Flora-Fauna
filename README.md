Wrath & Glory - Threat-Assessment: Flora & Fauna

Through the expanse of the Imperium, death can come as easily in the form of an Ork Choppa as a it can through the savage mandibles of a Sump-Spider. Entire worlds teem with hostile organisms, from the carnivorous vegetation and Megafauna of Nethreus to toxic fungal spores of an Underhive.

This homebrew supplement aims to lay out interesting encounters to support a GM fleshing out the world for their players, and will expand on the Battlezone rules from Threat Assessment Xenos as well as provide a broad range of non-factional threats for players to face.

This Foundry Module also includes a random Fauna Generator Macro, heavily based around the Rogue Trader tables found in The Koronus Bestiary supplement.
